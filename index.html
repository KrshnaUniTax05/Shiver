How Shiver Protects You

  • Your device mathematically converts your sentence into your account keys.
  • The server NEVER sees your sentence or your private keys.
  • All messages are End-to-End Encrypted (E2EE).
  • Message routing is anonymous. The server doesn't know who sends messages.
